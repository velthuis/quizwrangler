Remarkable Animals Field Quiz. Keep all questions together in one QTI ZIP.

1) TRUE/FALSE
A platypus is a mammal that lays eggs. TRUE.

2) Use sample-chart.png above the question. The chart shows dinosaur footprints
recorded at three field sites.
Which sequence of footprint counts matches the chart?
* 2, 4, and 6 footprints
* 1, 3, and 5 footprints
* 3, 6, and 9 footprints
Correct: first option.

3) Select every trait that applies to an axolotl. Please keep "axolotl" bold for students.
[x] It retains external gills as an adult
[x] It can regenerate lost limbs
[ ] It has feathers
Feedback: Axolotls retain external gills and can regenerate limbs; they do not have feathers.

4) Short answer with an inline equation: A museum display contains 2 real fossils and
2 replica fossils. Render the expression as MathML. How many objects are in the display?
Answer: 4.

5) Written response: Explain how an axolotl's external gills help it live in an aquatic habitat.
Show the prompt as a paragraph and give a ten-line answer box.

6) Match each formula from an aquatic-habitat lab to its name:
H2O -> water (the 2 must be subscript)
CO2 -> carbon dioxide (the 2 must be subscript)

7) Put these basic dinosaur life stages in order:
Egg, Hatchling, Adult.

8) Arithmetic. Each display case contains two dinosaur eggs. If the museum prepares
{n} display cases, how many eggs are displayed?
n is an integer from 2 through 10. Formula n*2. Precision 0. Tolerance 0 units.
Hint: Each display case contains <em>two</em> eggs.

9) Fill in the blank: An adult axolotl retains its external ______.
Answer: gills. Ignore capitalization.

10) Multi-short-answer: Name the two kinds of living monotremes. Two boxes.
Accept platypus or echidna in either box.
